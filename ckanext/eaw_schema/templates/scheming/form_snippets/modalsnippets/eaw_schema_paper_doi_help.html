Paper DOI

Enter the Digital Object Identifier (DOI) of the research paper or publication associated with this data package.

This is different from the dataset DOI above - this field is for the DOI of the scientific publication that this data supports.

Paper DOIs should be entered in the format: 10.1234/paper.identifier
Example: 10.1016/j.envsoft.2017.03.002
You can enter with or without the "https://doi.org/" prefix.
