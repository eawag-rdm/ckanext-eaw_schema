Authors

The author or the collective of authors taking scientific credit for the contents of the package, analogous to the author-list of an article.

Please use one of these formats to identify an author:

Person: Lastname, Firstname(s) <email@domainname.tld>

Institution: ABBREV: Full Institution Name

The first author must include an email address (unless it is an institution). Additional authors may omit the email, but the Lastname, Firstname format is always required for persons.

Ignore superfluous "Author <N>" fields. Empty ones will be discarded.

  At least one author is required.
