DOI

Enter the Digital Object Identifier (DOI) for this data package if one has been assigned.

A DOI is a persistent identifier that provides a reliable way to locate and cite digital content.

DOIs should be entered in the format: 10.1234/example.identifier
Examples: 10.1016/j.envsoft.2017.03.002 or 10.25678/000066
You can enter with or without the "https://doi.org/" prefix.
This is a mandatory field.
