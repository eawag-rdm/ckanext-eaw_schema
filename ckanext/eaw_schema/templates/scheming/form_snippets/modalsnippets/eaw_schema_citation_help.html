Dataset Citation

Enter the preferred citation format for this data package. This is how you would like others to cite your dataset in their publications.

If left empty, a citation will be auto-generated based on the metadata. You can use this field to provide a custom citation format or to override the auto-generated one.

Example citation format:
Author, A. (Year). Dataset Title. Eawag: Swiss Federal Institute of Aquatic Science and Technology. https://doi.org/10.xxxx/xxxxx
You can use Markdown formatting for emphasis, links, etc.
